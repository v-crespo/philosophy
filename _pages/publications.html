I work on AI ethics and PPE. My research focuses on how decision-makers should make choices about AI that affect the lives of current and future generations. First, I study how institutions that use predictive algorithms, such as banks, courts and hospitals, should compare alternative algorithms when deciding which one to use. Second, I study how to make policy choices, in areas such as AI governance, that may affect the size and well-being of future populations.
